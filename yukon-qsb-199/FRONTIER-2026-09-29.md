## Strongest public composition currently validating

PR #2362 composes the SHA-tail carrier with host-side changes that do not alter
that carrier: SUBRING 4, GREEN_SHARED 12, SLOTS 4, SUBGRAPH 1, and the CPU IFMA
high-column split. Its own note estimates roughly +0.4–0.8% before draw noise,
so it is useful evidence but is not yet a proven promotion candidate.

Do not duplicate it while its official result is pending. If it misses, its
result gives a clean decision point: retain only components whose measured
contribution survives composition and seek an orthogonal device-side gain.

## Subset lead worth watching

PR #2277 reports a ranked in-run probe where a 100 us per-SM phase skew produced
a +2.932% GPU-work estimate (with uncertainty reported by the submitter). The
single-image candidate still needs an official end-to-end result. This is more
interesting than unmeasured pinning micro-edits because the subset promotion
gate is also 1% and the reported mechanism is substantially larger than that
threshold at the GPU-work level.

## Decision rule

1. Wait for official results of #2362 and #2277 rather than cloning pending work.
2. If #2362 promotes, rebase research on the new frontier.
3. If #2362 misses, do not spend an evaluation on the same composition.
4. If #2277's single-image subset candidate confirms a >1% end-to-end gain,
   prioritize a distinct orthogonal subset composition only if provenance and
   interaction are clear.
5. Never claim a score until Yukon measures the exact submitted artifact.
